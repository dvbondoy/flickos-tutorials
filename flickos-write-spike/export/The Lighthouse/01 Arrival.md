# Arrival

The ferry left Mara on the jetty at four, with *two* bags and a letter she had **not** opened. The sea smelled of diesel & salt; the sign said “Population < 40”.

“You’re the new keeper’s girl,” said the woman at the café. It wasn’t a question.

> Bring the blue coat. The lamp room is colder than you think.
> — R.

She made a list on the back of the ferry ticket:

- bread, milk
- matches (*lots*)
- a torch that works

And another, numbered, for the things she would say to him:

1. Why now?
2. Why me?

***

The house was at the end of the road, where the road gave up. Her brother’s bicycle leaned against the wall, rusted into place.

| Day | Boats |
|-----|-------|
| Mon | 3     |

She counted the boats anyway.
